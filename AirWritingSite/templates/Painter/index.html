Why Use GestuRite?

Hands-Free Interaction

No keyboard. No pen. Simply write in the air and let AI capture your handwriting in real-time.

Gesture Controlled

Use simple finger gestures to write, undo, create sentences, clear canvas, and save.

Quick Guide

  • ✍️ Index Finger Up — Write characters in the air.
  • 👍 Thumb Up — Confirm current word.
  • ✌️ Two Fingers Up — Select buttons like Color, Delete, Mode, Next.
  • 👊 Fist — Clear the canvas instantly.
  • ➡️ Next Button — Saves the entire sentence for this session.
  • 📂 View Saved Documents — Opens all stored sentence files (auto-generated).
  • 📝 All words typed during camera session are saved automatically inside /saved_docs/.
